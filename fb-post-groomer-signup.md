## Note on the sign-up flow

Groomers get a real, working sign-up on the site — "List your business" creates their account and salon directly (name, address, phone, email/password) and takes them straight to the setup checklist at app.paw-booker.com, no waitlist or follow-up needed. The header's "Log in / Sign up" and the "For groomers" section both link there. Pet owners still see the email-capture waitlist modal, since customer booking isn't live on the web yet.
