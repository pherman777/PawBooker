PawBooker connects pet owners with trusted local groomers — and gives groomers one calm place to run their business.

FOR PET OWNERS
• Browse local groomers, compare services and pricing, and see real reviews
• Book appointments in a few taps, with reminders so you never miss one
• Add your pets' profiles (breed, size, notes) so groomers know what to expect
• Message your groomer directly and get help from an in-app AI assistant
• Pay securely in the app, including optional tips
• Manage, reschedule, or cancel upcoming bookings anytime

FOR GROOMERS
• Set your own services, pricing, and availability
• Manage bookings, staff, and your schedule from one dashboard
• Track earnings and business insights over time
• Chat with clients and get help from an AI business assistant
• Get paid directly through the app, commission-free on tips

PawBooker is a marketplace, not an employer — every groomer runs their own business and sets their own prices and hours. Whether you're booking a groom for your dog or cat, or running a grooming business, PawBooker keeps everything in one simple place.
